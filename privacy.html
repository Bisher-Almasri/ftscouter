FTC Scouter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

FTC Scouter ("the App") is provided by Bisher Almasri. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Summary

The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I do not collect, receive or store your personal data.

Data stored on your device

Scouting entries, notes, photos, settings and your alliance pick list are stored locally on your device. They never leave your device unless you choose to export or share them. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Network requests

To show event, team and match data the App sends read-only requests to the public FTCScout API (api.ftcscout.org) over HTTPS. These requests contain what you are looking up (for example an event code or team number), and, as with any internet request, your IP address is visible to that service. FTCScout's own privacy practices apply to those requests.

Photos and files

If you attach photos to scouting entries, they are stored locally in the App. The App only accesses photos or files you choose. Exporting and importing files is done by you, and you decide who receives them.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

I may update this policy and will change the date above when I do.
