FTC Scouter – Support

FTC Scouter is a scouting companion for FIRST Tech Challenge teams: offline match and pit scouting, match predictions, ranking forecasts and alliance pick lists.

Get help

Email bisherk.almasri@gmail.com with questions, bug reports or feature requests. Please include your device model, OS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account? No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking.

Where is my scouting data stored? Only on your device. Use the export option in the Scout tab to back it up or share it with teammates.

Does it work offline? Scouting forms work fully offline. Event, team and match data needs an internet connection to load from FTCScout, and is cached afterwards.

Where does the match data come from? The public FTCScout API (ftcscout.org). FTC Scouter is independent and is not affiliated with FIRST® or FTCScout.
